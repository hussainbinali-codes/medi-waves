Our Company

Medi Waves Inc.

The Company Medi Waves Inc. was established in the year 2000, with its head office located at the Industrial Hub of Delhi and manufacturing unit at HSIIDC Kundli, Sonepat, Haryana, enjoying the highly developed economic expansion and convenient transportation access.

We are combined with manufacturing, supply and service of Hospital Furniture, Emergency and Ward Equipment. We have our own state-of-the-art manufacturing units of various Medical, Hospital and Surgical instruments and equipment for Hospitals, Nursing Homes, Health Clinics, Physiotherapy Centres etc. and have a long list of satisfactory customers.
